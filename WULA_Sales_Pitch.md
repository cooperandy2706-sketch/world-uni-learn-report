Too often, schools rely on fragmented systems, Excel sheets, and paper trails that lead to data loss, stressed staff, unpaid arrears, and frustrated parents. 

**Nexora** is the definitive solution. It is a comprehensive, cloud-based School Management System engineered specifically for the Ghanaian educational landscape.

## The 11-Portal Ecosystem: A Comprehensive Breakdown

Nexora features strictly isolated, purpose-built dashboards tailored to the exact workflow of every staff member and stakeholder in your school.

### 1. 🏢 The Proprietor (Super Admin) Dashboard
*Strategic oversight for the school owner.*
- **Financial Analytics:** Real-time dashboards showing total revenue, outstanding arrears, and term-over-term growth.
- **Staff Performance Audits:** Monitor teacher attendance, syllabus coverage, and grading timelines.
- **Multi-School Management:** Overview of multiple branches (if applicable) from a single login.
- **System Audit Logs:** Track every major action taken within the system for absolute accountability.
- **Global News Management:** Push "Breaking News" or emergency alerts to all other portals simultaneously.

### 2. ⚙️ The School Admin Console
*The operational command center for the Headmaster or Principal.*
- **Admissions Management:** Streamlined onboarding for new students with automated ID generation.
- **Class & Subject Configuration:** Define classes, assign subjects, and set academic standards.
- **Staff Assignment:** Assign teachers to classes and bursars to specific financial roles.
- **Calendar & Timetabling:** Manage school events, holidays, and term dates.
- **Command Palette (⌘K):** Search for anything—students, staff, files, or settings—in milliseconds.

### 3. 👨‍🏫 The Teacher Portal
*Empowering educators to focus on the classroom.*
- **Digital Class Register:** Record morning and afternoon attendance with one tap.
- **CA & Exam Grading:** GES-compliant entry for Class Scores and Exams with automated grade calculations (A1-F9).
- **Automated Report Remarks:** Generate smart remarks based on student performance or enter custom conduct notes.
- **Syllabus Coverage Tracker:** Log daily progress against the national curriculum.
- **Video Assignments:** Assign educational videos (YouTube integration) and track student completion.
- **Lesson Plan Vault:** Create and store digital lesson plans for easy retrieval.

### 4. 💵 The Bursar & Finance Suite
*Maximizing revenue and ensuring financial integrity.*
- **Dynamic Billing Engine:** Set unique fee structures for boarding, day, and international students.
- **Debt Recovery Hub:** Filter students by "Amount Owed" and send bulk SMS fee reminders in seconds.
- **Instant SMS Receipts:** Parents receive a text message receipt the moment a payment is recorded.
- **Scholarship & Discount Management:** Apply partial or full scholarships to student accounts.
- **Expense & Vendor Tracking:** Record school purchases and manage external supplier accounts.
- **School Store/Inventory:** Sell uniforms, books, and stationery with real-time stock deductions.
- **Staff Payroll:** Manage basic salary disbursements and history.

### 5. 👨‍👩‍👧 The Parent Portal
*Transparency and peace of mind for every guardian.*
- **Real-Time Gradebook:** View child’s termly performance and continuous assessment progress.
- **Fee Transparency:** See exact balance due, payment history, and download digital receipts.
- **Attendance Alerts:** Get notified if a child is marked absent at school or on the bus.
- **Live Bus Tracking:** See the school bus location on a live map during morning and afternoon routes.
- **Direct Staff Messaging:** Securely message teachers or administrators regarding a child’s welfare.

### 6. 🎓 The Student Portal
*An interactive hub for the modern learner.*
- **AI Global Resources:** Access AI-generated textbooks, summaries, and rich media on any subject.
- **E-Assignments:** View and complete homework or video-based lessons.
- **Digital Report Cards:** Access and download current and past term reports.
- **Educational Games:** Improve skills with "Typing Nitro" and other built-in learning tools.
- **Student Council Elections:** Participate in secure, digital voting for school prefects.

### 7. 🛡️ The Security Portal
*Advanced safety and gate management.*
- **QR Gate Attendance:** Scan student ID cards at the gate to log entry/exit times.
- **Visitor Check-In System:** Record visitor ID details and purpose of visit.
- **Instant Visitor Badge Printing:** Print thermal badges with QR codes for all campus guests.
- **Incident Reporting:** Digitally log and track security or disciplinary incidents on campus.
- **Gate Analytics:** Monitor peak entry/exit times for better staff deployment.

### 8. 📚 The Library Management Portal
*Turning your library into a digital hub.*
- **Book Cataloging:** Digital database of all school library books.
- **QR Sticker Generation:** Print unique QR code labels to track physical book stock.
- **Rapid Mobile Scanning:** Use any smartphone to scan books for inventory reconciliation.
- **Lending & Returns:** Track borrowed books, due dates, and overdue fines.

### 9. 🚌 The Driver Portal
*Safe transport management.*
- **Route Navigation:** Designated paths for student pickups and drop-offs.
- **Boarding Manifests:** Scan students as they enter the bus to ensure no child is left behind.
- **Live Location Broadcasting:** Automatically updates the parent portal with the bus's current position.
- **Vehicle Logs:** Track fuel consumption and maintenance schedules.

### 10. 🏥 The Nurse / Health Portal
*Digitizing student health and wellness.*
- **Electronic Health Records (EHR):** Store student allergies, blood groups, and medical history.
- **Clinic Visit Logs:** Record every visit to the sickbay, symptoms, and treatments given.
- **Medication Inventory:** Manage the school’s pharmacy stock and expiry dates.
- **Emergency Health Alerts:** One-click notifications to parents in case of medical emergencies.

### 11. 🤖 The AI Content & Curriculum Portal
*Cutting-edge educational automation.*
- **AI Textbook Generator:** Create custom, detailed textbooks for any subject in seconds using Groq AI.
- **Media Library:** Curate high-quality educational videos and images for classroom use.
- **Curriculum Alignment:** Ensure all generated content meets national educational standards.
